# PP-105 empirical dataset and reported-result adapter research

Checked 28 October 2026. Research/proposal only: no implementation, dependency, fixture, ledger change, inference call or scientific verification. The first action was reading [GitHub issue 60](https://github.com/ManuelR-T/paperplay/issues/60), **PP-105: Explore empirical datasets, models and experimental outcomes**. Its scope is variable/group selection, missingness, replication, transformations and a reviewed subset of fits/summaries; its fallback is an annotated reported-result view when raw data are unavailable. Dependencies are PP082/083/084/090/092. This is not a request for another simulator or a general statistical package.

Recommendation: extend the existing checked-table/chart path with a small, deterministic **dataset study** module and a distinct **reported-result** mode. Initially support explicit group filters, complete-case descriptive summaries, declared-unit transformations and the existing unweighted straight-line least-squares fit. Preserve supplied uncertainty; defer computed confidence intervals, hypothesis tests, nonlinear/weighted fits, imputation and automatic model selection. All reading/analysis remains local and provider-free. No frozen held-out source is used.

- `lib/representations/statistical-examples.ts` and `data/statistical-examples.json` already contain the small PP090 plant/NOAA extracts. `tests/statistical-charts.test.ts` covers basic fits, provenance, missingness and chart restoration. - `shared/research-evidence.ts`, `lib/representations/evidence.ts`, `lib/representations/registry.ts` and `lib/representations/data-study.ts` provide declarations, source-bound adapter execution and portable local study envelopes. Declared/imported results cannot mint trusted scientific receipts. Keep that boundary and preserve existing v1/v2 study imports.

**Frozen development-corpus case B1: Hodgkin–Huxley reported-result mode.** A. L. Hodgkin and A. F. **Existing physical counterexample:** [NOAA's monthly Mauna Loa product explanation](https://gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/trends/mlo.html), paragraphs below the graphs, distinguishes monthly means, seasonal adjustment and uncertainty accounting for dependence between successive days. Its contributing-day count is not independent experimental replication; do not compute `daily SD / sqrt(days)` as replacement uncertainty. ## Scientific semantics and bounded methods

The following are proposed adapter policies, informed by primary definitions rather than claims that the source approves this design.

| Concern | Minimal policy and primary basis |
| --- | --- |
| Replication | Store observation-unit description, experimental-unit description, optional unit-ID column, optional technical/repeated-measure ID, supplied n and its meaning separately. Unknown is explicit. Row count, nonmissing count, distinct unit count and source-reported n are different outputs. The [NC3Rs Experimental Design Assistant](https://eda.nc3rs.org.uk/experimental-design-unit) defines independent intervention units; repeated measurements do not create more units. This design principle applies beyond its animal examples, but the source does not establish the plant experiment's independence. |
| Missingness | Show total/selected/usable/missing counts per selected variable and per joint fit. Default complete-case analysis only, recording excluded row IDs and reason. Never zero-fill, interpolate, infer missing-at-random or silently delete a group. Missingness can invalidate inference; numerical completion alone establishes no assumption. |
| Descriptive summaries | Per selected group: finite n, min/max, arithmetic mean and sample SD using denominator n−1. n=0 gives unavailable mean/SD; n=1 gives mean but unavailable SD. [NIST §1.3.5.6](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda356.htm) defines variance/SD and distinguishes units of variance from SD. Summaries of source means are labeled summaries of means, never pooled raw observations. |
| SEM and intervals | Carry source-supplied SD/SE/intervals with their interpretation and coverage if supplied. A recomputed SEM requires a declared independent-unit analysis and n≥2; otherwise mark unavailable. n=1 is not zero uncertainty. [NIST §1.3.5.2](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda352.htm) distinguishes mean uncertainty and a t interval's long-run coverage; ±1 SE is not automatically 95% CI. Initial scope need not implement t quantiles or inferred intervals. |
| OLS | Reuse the unweighted intercept+slope model only with ≥2 complete pairs and varying finite x. Retain row IDs, residuals, SSE, fitted domain, x/y units and method version. n=2 can define a line but cannot estimate residual variance with n−2 degrees of freedom. [NIST §4.1.4.1](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmd/section1/pmd141.htm) defines least-squares minimization and notes outlier/extrapolation risks. A descriptive line does not require a normality claim; inferential statements require additional assumptions. |
| Assumptions | Show declared/unknown/contradicted rather than “assumptions passed” based on checkbox or small sample. Independence, error structure and predictor error are not established by regression execution. [NIST process-model assumptions](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmd/section2/pmd21.htm) explain why applicability matters. No p values, causal effects or automatic test choice in this increment. |
| Transformations | Enumerated trusted operations only: explicit compatible-unit conversion; optionally log of positive values relative to a declared reference unit/value. An axis drawn logarithmically is not a log-transformed fit. Record operation/order/version and omitted domain-invalid rows or reject explicitly. Absolute Celsius points convert affinely; SD/SE/differences convert without offsets. Do not take log of raw dimensional values or silently convert calendar coordinates to seconds. |

Keep three result badges that cannot be changed by display settings: **reported by source** (source text/table), **recomputed locally** (method+exact input binding), **illustrative reconstruction** (authored assumptions). Processed source values and model predictions retain their separate origins beneath those badges. Uncertainty type is independent of origin: a source report may contain no uncertainty at all.

## Proposed contract and module boundary

Add a narrow versioned `DatasetStudyRequest`/`DatasetStudyResult` contract, not arbitrary formula execution. Suggested request fields: exact source fingerprint, checked table ID+checksum or explicit unavailable-data reason, variable/group selection, trusted transformation list, analysis enum (`descriptive`/`linear-ols`), explicit replication declaration, source result cards, protocol references and objective. Numeric source result cards need estimand, value/unit, uncertainty meaning if supplied, locator and availability; qualitative cards need no fake numeric table. Objective should be authored/declared (inspect values, compare groups descriptively, inspect reported result), distinct from any source hypothesis.

Suggested deep module `lib/datasets/model.ts`: `validateDatasetStudyRequest`, `analyzeDatasetStudy`, `validateDatasetStudyResult(result,request)`. Return normalized selections/units, exact included and excluded row IDs, missingness, replicate audit, summaries/fit/residuals, diagnostics and immutable bindings. A `reported-only` result requires no asset and enables source/protocol navigation while numerical controls remain unavailable. Fit failure must retain observations and diagnostics rather than downgrade them into reconstructed data. Keep rendering/selection and source navigation outside the numerical module.

Bind outputs to source fingerprint, input checksum, selected rows/columns/groups, transformation order/options, unit policy, method version and replication declaration. Changing those invalidates computed outputs; camera/layout/navigation changes do not. Result validation must reject substituted bindings, groups, counts, methods, nonfinite statistics and false completeness. Serialized results are declarations until recomputed/checked locally; imported JSON never restores a WeakSet receipt. Export exact inputs only within existing explicit local-download/privacy choices; unknown rights are retained, not converted to permission. Do not introduce a new unchecked dataset store.

Conservative bounds can reuse the existing 1,000-row/32-column asset ceiling, ≤8 selected groups, ≤16 trusted transformations, ≤2 fit variables, bounded row-level diagnostics and output bytes. Validate bounds before traversing input; no unrestricted join, expression, download, model search or dependency is needed. A worker is optional for this bounded arithmetic, but cancellation/stale-response rejection and no automatic paid creation remain necessary. Registry registration should reuse the scientific adapter/lifecycle path and leave existing statistical charts/imports working.

## Verification and admission plan

1. **Independent numeric oracle:** [NIST StRD Norris original data and certified values](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/strd/lls/data/LINKS/DATA/Norris.dat) supplies 36 observed calibration pairs and certified OLS intercept `−0.262323073774029`, slope `1.00211681802045`, SSE `26.6173985294224`. Header does not specify physical units: use an explicitly unspecified-unit algorithm conformance test, not a physical claim. Downloaded only to `/tmp/paperplay-pp105-Norris.dat`: 2,688 bytes, SHA-256 `5ab6906c68a9c4b2eb1661c0cd7d2a09f5a469a6d4e05b6b691f190f5f7b944f`. Certified values were inspected, not recomputed in this research. Before bundling bytes, establish the dataset's redistribution terms; public access alone is insufficient. A wholly authored rational fixture can cover routine CI without this admission dependency.
2. **Small independent references:** authored `[1,2,3]` has mean 2, sample SD 1, SEM `1/sqrt(3)` only under the declared independent-unit interpretation. Authored pairs `(0,1),(1,3),(2,5)` yield intercept 1/slope 2/SSE 0; label synthetic. Test all-null, singleton, constant x, overflow, huge offsets, incompatible units, positive-domain violations and n=2 residual-uncertainty unavailability. Compare numerical tolerances justified by float precision; do not expect decimal bit equality.
3. **Replication and uncertainty:** same plant/unit repeated over ten ranks must not become n=10 independent plants. Duplicate/missing pairing IDs, unequal group sizes and unavailable n must stay explicit. Source SE cannot become SD/CI; supplied asymmetric intervals remain asymmetric; pooled subgroup means cannot become raw pooled samples. Test affine absolute-temperature conversion separately from spread conversion.
4. **Integration and recovery:** plant filters/variable changes update missingness and bound summaries; NOAA daily counts do not enable SEM inference. B1 opens a useful source-linked reported-only card with no invented table, fit or protocol completeness. Exact restored inputs reproduce outputs; changed source/data/method invalidate them. Unknown/corrupt versions preserve original bytes for recovery, never silently migrate to trusted results.
5. **Native browser:** use keyboard/mobile to select variables/groups, inspect missing rows and n definitions, distinguish source/recomputed/illustrative panels, follow HH result→partial protocol→source, save/reopen locally, and see an unavailable-data explanation. No network inference on mount/filter/reopen, no paid CI and no claim of a human reader study or scientific replication.

Open decisions for implementation: whether SEM is included initially or only source SEM; whether to expose log transforms initially; whether reported cards extend the dataset view or reuse an existing evidence view. These do not require broadening statistical scope. Computed confidence/prediction intervals, weighted/multilevel fits and formal hypothesis tests need separate method-specific contracts and independent references before admission.
