# Meta hackathon requirements — checked 27 October 2026

Primary sources: the [official event page](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon), its publicly delivered embedded Event Waiver/Official Rules, and the official [submission target](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit). This file paraphrases their operational requirements; it is not a copy of the rules or a substitute for the participant accepting them.

## Verified facts relevant to Paperplay

- The published project deadline is **4 November, 9:00 PM US Eastern Time**. For the published 2026 schedule, this is 5 November at 03:00 in Paris. Organizers may change dates; recheck immediately before submitting.
- This is a virtual event; a project enters one track. The individual-builder/student track is the relevant published track for a solo developer. Eligibility/acceptance is the participant's own account state; no acceptance is inferred here.
- Meta products are the event's tooling basis. Paperplay's actual official Meta inference integration should be shown in the demonstration, with deterministic local simulations clearly identified separately.
- The embedded rules give project judging weights: impact 30%, use of Meta AI tooling 25%, technical depth/execution 20%, demo 25%. These project criteria are distinct from application-screening criteria shown elsewhere on the event page.
- Selected participants' published Meta Model API allowance is $150 for a ten-day development window. Paperplay cannot infer remaining credit from that advertised allowance; its token ledger is separate from provider billing.
- The rules permit building on open-source components subject to their licenses and require rights in submitted material. Keep the project's attribution inventory and licensed-paper notices. No open-source license transfers the authors' scientific claims to Paperplay.
- The rules name the submission portal linked above and include the quality of a demo video in judging.

## Not publicly verified

Anonymous access to `/details` returned a loading/sign-in shell. Anonymous access to the submission target exposed the public event rather than an authenticated upload form. No submission was made, and no messages were sent.

The inspected public page/rules do **not** establish a maximum video length, upload size/format, whether repository visibility is mandatory, required screenshot fields, or the exact accepted participant's submission form. A two-minute script can be a useful editorial choice, but must not be presented as a verified two-minute portal rule. Do not reuse requirements from another Cerebral Valley event.

## Concrete handoff checklist

1. In the participant's accepted account, open the submission portal and confirm the current fields, video limits, URL/repository access requirements and final deadline.
2. Use the prepared English demo material, showing one meaningful input change, deterministic result/comparison, source return, and one genuine Meta-enriched import or saved live result with honest reuse labeling.
3. Show model/provider integration, source privacy, scientific limitations and graceful provider-failure fallback accurately. Do not call conceptual step navigation an execution of an unsupported algorithm.
4. Attach the actual hosted URL, repository access and real recorded video/artifacts requested by the authenticated form. Do not substitute a localhost URL if the form requires hosting.
5. Review the final materials and submit manually only when the participant intends to enter. This research and the local issue plan do not perform or authorize a submission on their own.
